Privacy Policy

Routebound · Last updated: April 28, 2026

Routebound is a mobile puzzle game. This policy explains what data may be processed when you use the app.

Information We Collect

Routebound does not require an account and does not ask you to create public user content. The app may use Firebase and Google AdMob services to operate, improve stability, and show ads.

  • Game progress stored on device: cleared levels, best unlocked level, daily streak, settings, hint usage, and clean-flow records may be stored locally on your device.
  • Analytics and app interactions: Firebase Analytics may process app events such as level starts, clears, hints, settings changes, and onboarding flow performance.
  • Diagnostics: Firebase Crashlytics may process crash logs, device/app state, and diagnostic information to fix bugs.
  • Remote configuration: Firebase Remote Config may be used to tune non-sensitive app behavior such as onboarding, difficulty, or ad policy settings.
  • Advertising data: Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform may process advertising identifiers, device information, consent signals, and ad interaction data where allowed by your consent choices and local law.

How We Use Information

  • To provide core puzzle gameplay and save local progress
  • To improve onboarding, level balance, stability, and performance
  • To understand whether ads are disruptive and keep ads away from the puzzle board
  • To provide optional rewarded hint ads
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and invalid advertising activity

Advertising

Routebound uses Google AdMob. Ads are designed to stay outside the puzzle board and avoid interrupting the early learning flow. Rewarded hints are optional. Google may process advertising data according to your consent choices, device settings, and Google policies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data may be processed by service providers such as Google Firebase and Google AdMob under their own terms and privacy policies when those SDKs are active in the app.

Retention And Deletion

Local game progress remains on your device unless you delete the app or reset local data through available app or device controls. Third-party service data is retained according to the relevant provider policies.

Children

Routebound is intended as a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under the age required by local law for consent-based data processing.
