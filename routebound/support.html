Routebound is a calm arrow-order puzzle game. This page explains the core rule and how to contact support.

How To Play

  • Tap an arrow to send it in the direction it points.
  • If the lane is open, the arrow leaves the board.
  • If another arrow blocks the way, it bumps back and stays.
  • Clear every arrow by finding the right order.

Common Questions

The arrow did not leave the board. Why?
Another arrow, lock, or route object is blocking its path. Try a different arrow first, or use Hint if you want the next safe move.

Does Routebound require payment?
No. Routebound uses AdMob advertising and does not include subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Can I change the language?
Yes. Routebound follows the system language by default and supports English and Korean. You can also change the language from Settings.

Do ads cover the puzzle board?
The ad policy is designed to keep ads outside the board and avoid forced ads during the early learning flow. Rewarded hint ads are optional.
