Threadlight Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-30

Threadlight is a mobile puzzle game. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use the app.

Information Processed

  • Gameplay and app usage data: level starts, level completions, hints, restarts, session events, and similar interaction events may be processed to improve game balance and reliability.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and performance signals may be processed to identify and fix technical issues.
  • Advertising data: because the app uses Google AdMob, ad identifiers and ad interaction signals may be processed by Google to provide and measure ads.
  • Local progress: level progress and settings are stored on your device so the game can restore your state.

Services Used

The app may use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config, and Google AdMob. These services are provided by Google and may process data according to Google's policies.

How Information Is Used

  • To provide puzzle gameplay, progress saving, settings, hints, and daily challenge behavior.
  • To understand which levels are too easy, too hard, or causing failures.
  • To improve stability and diagnose crashes.
  • To show ads in a way that supports the free app while avoiding intrusive gameplay placement.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by service providers such as Google Firebase and Google AdMob to operate analytics, diagnostics, remote configuration, and advertising features.

Children

Threadlight is designed as a general-audience puzzle game. It does not knowingly request children to provide personal information directly in the app.
