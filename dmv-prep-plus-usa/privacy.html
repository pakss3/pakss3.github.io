Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-03-08

This Privacy Policy explains how PermitBeacon USA handles information.

Information We Collect

  • Local study data: selected state, language, exam date, practice history, wrong-answer recovery, mock history, and rewarded unlock state may be stored on-device.
  • App usage and diagnostics: Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics may collect limited usage and diagnostics data when enabled in the shipped build.
  • Ads and consent: Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform may process advertising identifiers, device information, consent signals, and ad interaction data when ads are enabled.

How We Use Information

  • To provide state-specific practice, wrong-answer review, readiness coaching, and store-compliant ad delivery
  • To improve reliability and performance
  • To respect privacy choices before requesting ads

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. If third-party services are used, data may be processed by those providers under their own policies.

Retention and Deletion

Study data is stored locally on the device unless a third-party SDK collects limited analytics or diagnostics data. Users can reset local study data from the in-app Trust Center.

Children

PermitBeacon USA is not directed to children under 13.
