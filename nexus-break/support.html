Support and Review Information

NEXUS BREAK is a neon chain-reaction puzzle game. This page provides the public support surface used for App Store registration, review follow-up, and player issue contact.

Before you contact support

  • Check that you are on the latest App Store build available to you.
  • Include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue.
  • If the issue involves ads or progression, mention the exact screen where it happened.

App Review notes

  • No login is required to access the core game flow.
  • The app is designed around drag, preview, and release puzzle gameplay.
  • Advertising is limited to non-gameplay surfaces and optional rewarded flows.
  • No paid digital content is required to access the current gameplay loop.
