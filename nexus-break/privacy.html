Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-18. This Privacy Policy explains how NEXUS BREAK handles information for iPhone users.

Core app data

NEXUS BREAK stores gameplay-related information such as settings, progression, and local state on your device so the puzzle experience can continue between sessions.

Advertising and third-party services

The app includes Google Mobile Ads for banner and optional rewarded ad placements. If ad features are enabled in the production build, Google and its partners may process device identifiers, app interaction signals, and diagnostic information according to their own privacy terms.

The app also includes Firebase platform services used for mobile app bootstrap and related reliability support. If enabled by the shipped build, diagnostic or service configuration data may be processed by Google Firebase.

Data sharing and purchases

We do not sell personal information. The current gameplay flow does not require account login, and paid digital content is not required to access the core puzzle loop described on the public support page.

Your choices

  • You can stop using the app at any time and remove app data by uninstalling it from your device.
  • You can decline optional ad interactions by not starting rewarded ad flows.
  • You can contact us if you have privacy questions or need clarification.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact pakss3@naver.com.
