Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-01

This Privacy Policy explains how Kaiwa Quest handles information.

Information We Collect

  • Learning progress and settings: selected support lane, saved expressions, review state, and scene progress may be stored on-device.
  • App usage and diagnostics: Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics may collect limited usage and diagnostics data when enabled in the shipped build.
  • Ads and consent: Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform may process advertising identifiers, device information, consent signals, and ad interaction data when ads are enabled.
  • Microphone access: microphone permission is requested only for speaking and roleplay features that require learner audio input.

How We Use Information

  • To provide lesson playback, speaking practice, review drills, and saved-expression features
  • To improve reliability, performance, and learning quality
  • To respect privacy choices before requesting ads when consent flows are available

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. If third-party services are used, data may be processed by those providers under their own policies.

Retention and Deletion

Study data is primarily stored locally on the device unless a third-party SDK collects limited analytics, diagnostics, or advertising-related data. Users can remove local app data by deleting the app from their device.

Children

Kaiwa Quest is not directed to children under 13.
