Focus Friend KR Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-22

Focus Friend KR is a local-first focus timer and companion room app. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information Stored on Your Device

  • Focus sessions: session start and end time, planned duration, actual duration, mode, category, optional goal note, completion state, interruption count, reward amount, and selected ambient sound.
  • Settings and goals: notification preferences, sound preferences, theme choice, daily focus goal, weekly goal, and week start day.
  • Room and companion progress: selected companion, owned items, room theme, applied decor, closet items, streak, and Focus Star balance.

Local-First Design

The app does not require an account. The core focus, reward, room, and settings data described above is stored locally on your device so the app can restore your progress and preferences.

Notifications and Audio

If you allow notifications, the app may schedule local notifications for focus completion, break reminders, and gentle routine prompts. Ambient sounds are played locally in the app. You can change notification and sound preferences in the app or in your device settings.

Data Collection

The current release does not operate a Focus Friend KR server account system and does not transmit your focus records to our servers. The app is designed to work without a network connection for its core features.

Third-Party Services

The current release does not include third-party advertising SDKs or mandatory login services. If analytics, crash reporting, cloud backup, payments, or advertising are added in a future release, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before those features are enabled.

Children

Focus Friend KR is a general productivity app. It does not knowingly request children to provide personal information directly in the app.

Data Deletion

You can delete locally stored app data by deleting the app from your device. If future account or backup features are added, additional deletion options will be provided.
