Rift Lantern Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 28, 2026

Rift Lantern does not require an account to play. Gameplay progress is stored locally on your device. The app may use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config, and Google AdMob rewarded ads.

Information Collected

Usage data. The app may collect gameplay and product interaction events such as app launches, floor starts, floor completions, rewarded revive prompts, ad reward completion, and feature usage.

Diagnostics. Crash logs, performance diagnostics, device model, operating system version, app version, and similar technical information may be collected to fix crashes and improve reliability.

Advertising data and identifiers. If rewarded ads are enabled, Google AdMob may process advertising-related data, including ad interaction data and device identifiers, according to user consent, platform permissions, and Google's policies.

Local gameplay progress. Floor progress, upgrades, relics, daily/weekly objectives, and similar game state are stored locally on your device.

Information Not Collected

Rift Lantern does not collect contacts, photos, precise location, health data, financial data, or private messages.

How Information Is Used

  • To provide and improve the app experience.
  • To analyze gameplay balance and retention.
  • To diagnose crashes, bugs, and performance problems.
  • To deliver optional rewarded ads and verify ad rewards.
  • To prevent abuse and maintain service reliability.

Third-Party Services

Rift Lantern may use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config, and Google AdMob. These services may process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Tracking and Consent

Where required by law or platform policy, advertising or analytics behavior may depend on consent choices. If personalized advertising or cross-app tracking is enabled in a future build, the app must request the appropriate user permission and App Store Connect privacy answers must be updated before release.

Children

Rift Lantern is not directed to children under 13. The app should not be used by children where parental consent is required by local law.

Contact
