The app stores your nickname, lesson progress, review schedule, streak, reward unlocks, and study preferences on your device.

2. How We Use It

This information is used to reopen learning where you left off and to maintain anonymous install-level operational records.

3. Third-Party Services

HolaQuest may rely on AdMob, Firebase, Firestore, and device speech recognition under their own platform policies.

You can change nickname, language, and audio settings in the app, and use Reset learning data to erase local route progress. That reset cannot be undone.
