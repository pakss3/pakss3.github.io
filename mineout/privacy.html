Mineout Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Mineout mobile app for iPhone.

Information we collect

Mineout does not require account creation and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location through the app.

Local app data

The app stores gameplay progress, unlocked content, and settings locally on your device so the app can remember your experience between sessions.

Analytics and advertising

At the time of this policy update, Mineout functions without mandatory sign-in and without requiring you to provide personal information to play the core game. If analytics, advertising, or online features are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before those changes are released.

Children's privacy

Mineout is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13 for the purpose of collecting personal information.

Your choices

You may delete locally stored gameplay data by removing the app from your device.

Contact

If you have any privacy questions, contact pakss3@nate.com.
