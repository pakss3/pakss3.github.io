Privacy Policy

Emberglass Apprentice · Last updated: July 2, 2026

Emberglass Apprentice is an offline 2D platform game. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Data Collection

Emberglass Apprentice does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal data. The app does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, user accounts, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

The app may store game progress and settings locally on your device, such as completed sections, unlocked progress, and control or audio preferences. This local data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Network And Accounts

The app does not require login, account creation, or an internet connection for gameplay. If a future version adds an online feature, this policy will be updated before that feature is released.

Children

Emberglass Apprentice is a general-audience game and is not designed to collect information from children. Because the app does not collect personal data, we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
