Emberglass Apprentice is an offline 2D platform game set in a moonlit alchemy citadel.

How To Play

  • Use the on-screen left and right controls to move.
  • Use the jump button to cross gaps, avoid hazards, and reach platforms.
  • Explore the citadel, avoid sentries, and activate alchemy devices as you progress.
  • Your progress is stored locally on your device.

Common Questions

Does the game require an internet connection?
No. Emberglass Apprentice is playable offline.

Does the game include ads or in-app purchases?
No. The current release has no ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

How do I reset progress?
Delete and reinstall the app to clear local game data from the device.
