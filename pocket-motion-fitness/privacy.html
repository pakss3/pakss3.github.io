Pocket Motion Fitness Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

Pocket Motion Fitness is designed as a camera-free, phone-in-pocket workout companion. This policy explains how the app handles data.

Data Collection

The app does not collect data from users for tracking, advertising, analytics, data broker sale, or developer-operated server storage.

On-Device Motion Processing

Workout motion signals may be processed on the device to estimate exercise state, confidence, and repetition progress. These signals are used locally for app functionality and are not sent to the developer.

Accounts and Login

The app does not require account creation or login.

Health, Fitness, and Permissions

The app may request motion-related permissions to provide workout feedback. Permission use is limited to app functionality. The app is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or rehabilitation advice.

Third Parties

The app does not use third-party advertising SDKs or developer-operated analytics collection.
