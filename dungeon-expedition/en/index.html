Dungeon Expedition · Pixel Roguelite RPG

Every floor begins
a new expedition.

Move your hero with one hand while basic attacks happen automatically. Choose skills and gear that change from run to run as you push deeper into a pixel-art dungeon.

Build a new strategy on every run, combine weapons and companions, and face the boss waiting on each floor. Rest at camp every ten floors before the next challenge.
