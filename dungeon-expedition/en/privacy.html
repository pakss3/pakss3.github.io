Privacy Policy

Effective: October 8, 2026 · Operator: Sangsu Park

Learn what Dungeon Expedition processes for advertising, analytics, and crash diagnostics, and what choices are available.

1. Accounts and on-device storage

The game does not require an account or ask you to enter your name, email address, or phone number. Game progress and settings are stored on your device and are not synced to a developer server. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage may remove this local data.

2. Advertising and consent

The game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to serve and measure ads and help prevent abuse. For advertising, Google may process or share with advertising providers your IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), advertising or device identifiers, ad views and interactions, app and device information, and performance and diagnostic data. Purposes and ad personalization can depend on your region, consent, and device privacy settings. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) displays a consent form where one is required. See Google advertising privacy information.

3. Optional usage analytics

Google Firebase Analytics is off by default. It is enabled only if you turn analytics on in game settings. It may process gameplay screen and feature events and technical information such as app version and platform. Turning the setting off stops subsequent Analytics collection. This choice is separate from advertising consent.

4. Crash and performance diagnostics

Google Firebase Crashlytics automatically processes crash and error reports and performance and diagnostic information to help improve stability. Reports may include error and stack details, timestamps, app version, device model and operating system, and Google-generated app installation and session identifiers. The current app does not provide a setting to disable Crashlytics collection. See Firebase privacy and security and Crashlytics documentation.

5. Third-party services and retention

Advertising, analytics, and diagnostic information may be sent to Google service providers and handled under Google’s policies and retention practices. The developer does not separately collect gameplay progress on a developer server or sell the information described here. For exact retention periods and choices, see each provider’s information.

6. Children and guardians

The game does not ask you to enter direct identifying information. Information processed by advertising and diagnostic SDKs is described above. A parent or guardian with a privacy question or concern can contact us below.

7. Contact and changes

For privacy questions, email pakss328@gmail.com. We will update this page and its effective date if the service or applicable requirements change.
